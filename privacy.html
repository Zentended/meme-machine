Privacy notes

This is the 0.6 build. The starter catalog matches words in your browser. This build has no sign-in, analytics, query history, or audio recorder. Media selected from your device plays locally and is not uploaded.

Voice starts only when you tap the microphone. Your browser or its speech service may handle speech remotely. That service’s settings and privacy terms apply. You can type instead.

If a site operator enables GIPHY, unmatched GIF searches send the search text to GIPHY directly. Opening or sharing an original source uses the destination’s own service. Do not put private information in search terms. Hosting providers may keep ordinary access logs.

The web app can cache its own files for offline use and remember the fullscreen setting. Clear browser site data to remove them.
