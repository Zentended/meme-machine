# Starter media: Apollo 11 reaction pack

All clips are excerpts of recordings in [NASA HEASARC’s Historical Videos archive](https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/heasarc/videos/historical.html), with their own contemporaneous audio. Resized and transcoded to H.264/AAC for mobile; no synthetic dialogue or replacement soundtrack. GIFs are silent adaptations. These five items are a starter pack, not a comprehensive pop-culture library.
